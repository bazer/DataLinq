## Bounded source findings

The capability review inspects `MissingCapability_IsExplicit_AndDoesNotInferSupportFromDbCommand`, validation/pre-cancellation controls, `SuspendedDispatch_ReceivesOriginalToken_AndCancelsWithoutSyncFallback` and `SuspendedProviderFailure_KeepsOriginalException_WithoutReplay`. The interfaces require explicit support; ordinary DbCommand async members do not grant that support.

The owner review inspects `SuspendedOperation_RejectsAnotherCallWithoutDisturbingItsOwner`, `Lease_CanResumeOnAnotherThread_WithoutGrantingPublicAdmission` and `ActiveStep_RejectsOverlapReleaseAndTransfer_WithoutChangingOwnership`. The private-dispatch tests `SyncOwnedCommands_RejectForeignRetiredAndUnboundAuthorityBeforeDispatch` and `SyncOwnedCommands_DefaultHooksPreservePublicOverridesAndBorrowedCommands` exercise actual override dispatch rather than substituting a compatibility assertion on a mock interface. The helper gate closes admission under its lock, then awaits/drains outside the lock without canceling or abandoning escaped work. That source inspection does not replace all helper-path assertions.

A current `Task.Run` / `Task.Factory.StartNew` / `GetAwaiter().GetResult()` / `.Wait(` scan of core and Memory was followed by inspection of the matches. [CacheCleanupScheduler](../../../../src/DataLinq/Cache/CacheCleanupScheduler.cs) starts and synchronously joins its owned maintenance task during stop; [ThreadWorker](../../../../src/DataLinq/Workers/ThreadWorker.cs) provides an existing long-running worker. [ImmutableRelation](../../../../src/DataLinq/Instances/ImmutableRelation.cs) and [ImmutableForeignKey](../../../../src/DataLinq/Instances/ImmutableForeignKey.cs) wait for the shared load slot from synchronous entry points. These are maintenance/coordination boundaries, not wrappers that dispatch provider synchronous I/O through Task.Run. No such facade was found by this bounded scan. Provider files and their later native implementation remain subject to the full W2 audit.

### F04 — Initialization publication and terminal failure

[LazyTransactionResource](../../../../src/DataLinq/Execution/LazyTransactionResource.cs) publishes Ready only after initialization finishes, captures the primary before cleanup, retains a resource whose cleanup failed for later disposal, and never resets Failed to Unused. An early cancellation leaves Unused; successful initialization followed by cancellation leaves Ready. A private owner does not authorize overlapping initialization/disposal calls.

[LazyTransactionResourceTests](../../../../src/DataLinq.Tests.Unit/Core/LazyTransactionResourceTests.cs) exercise open/configure/begin suspension, failure and cancellation, shared sync/async first use, no replay and cleanup retention. `ResourcesRemainPrivateUntilEveryInitializationStageSucceeds` and `InterruptedInitialization_IsTerminal_AndRetainsOwnershipUntilCleanup` inspect state, publication, calls and competing admission while suspended.

[Managed reader integration](../../../../src/DataLinq.Tests.Unit/Core/TransactionMutationFailureTests.Initialization.cs) distinguishes pre-admission, pre-initialization and post-success cancellation; `InitializedReader_UnfinishedHelper_DrainsInitializationAndRetainedResourceWithoutCommitOrRollback` checks the full retained-resource failure order. [Occurrence controls](../../../../src/DataLinq.Tests.Unit/Core/TransactionMutationFailureTests.InitializationOccurrences.cs), especially `SettledOccurrences_InitializationKeepsPrimaryBeforeCleanup`, verify sync/async original stack and immutable context even when cleanup rethrows the same exception.

The [reader](../../../../src/DataLinq/Execution/InitializingTransactionReaderSource.cs), [scalar](../../../../src/DataLinq/Execution/InitializingTransactionScalarSource.cs) and [eager command](../../../../src/DataLinq/Execution/AsyncEagerCommand.cs) adapters carry the same owner, validate before cancellation, check again before the application statement, and report failed initialization as dispose-only. `AsyncQueryPlan_InitializationFailureIsTerminalBeforeScalarOrProjectionDispatch` and `AsyncMutation_InitializationFailureHasNoStatementButRemainsDisposeOnly` prove no statement creation/dispatch through these callers. Native open/setup/begin and concrete provider resource publication remain W2.

### F06 — Trust, completion and immutable recovery facts

[ExecutionRecoveryPolicy and ExecutionFailures](../../../../src/DataLinq/Execution/ExecutionFailure.cs) require successful cleanup plus explicit confirmed integrity and ordinary-read/no-statement effects before Continue. Initialization, lost integrity or failed cleanup permit disposal only; rollback requires its own evidence. No-dispatch normalization preserves initialization/lost restrictions. [Transaction](../../../../src/DataLinq/Mutation/Transaction.cs) enforces the recovery snapshot as well as ownership; a free gate is not evidence of a usable transaction.

`RecoveryRequiresEvidenceAndCleanup`, `RecoveryCannotRewriteEstablishedCompletion` and `OrderedSecondarySnapshotsPreserveExceptionsAndPrimaryStack` in [ExecutionFailureTests](../../../../src/DataLinq.Tests.Unit/Core/ExecutionFailureTests.cs) check the matrix, immutable ordered copies, exception identity and original stack. [Read recovery tests](../../../../src/DataLinq.Tests.Unit/Core/TransactionMutationFailureTests.ReadRecovery.cs) enforce it through actual managed reads/writes/commit/rollback, preserve prior work on trusted local cancellation, reject unrelated exception relabeling, and permit source fallback only after confirmed completion.

[Managed async completion](../../../../src/DataLinq/Mutation/Transaction.AsyncCompletion.cs) separates provider completion, local finalization and notifications. `AsyncUncertainCommit_RemainsUnknownAfterConfirmedRollback_AndRejectsBusinessWork`, `AsyncCommittedCacheFailure_PreservesSpecialExceptionAndNeverRollsBack` and `AsyncFailedRollback_AllowsOnlyDisposal_WithoutRollbackRetry` in [completion tests](../../../../src/DataLinq.Tests.Unit/Core/TransactionMutationFailureTests.AsyncCompletion.cs) prove the decisive terminal rules. The common model and these consumers are reviewed; mutation-specific effects and complete classification/telemetry reconciliation remain F09/F20. Actual provider trust/certainty still requires W2.

### F07 — Independent cooperative recovery budget

[AutomaticTransactionRecovery](../../../../src/DataLinq/Execution/AutomaticTransactionRecovery.cs) accepts no request token, transfers only an idle owner, starts one budget at rollback, awaits actual completion, and attempts transaction and connection disposal independently. Expiry requests cancellation; it never abandons work, returns a still-active connection or retries. [RecoveryRollbackSettings](../../../../src/DataLinq/Execution/RecoveryRollbackSettings.cs) captures 30 seconds by default and validates the accepted 1 through 4,294,967,294 millisecond interval.

All substantive assertions in [AutomaticTransactionRecoveryTests](../../../../src/DataLinq.Tests.Unit/Core/AutomaticTransactionRecoveryTests.cs) were inspected, including `ActiveWorkMustSettleBeforeTransfer_AndBudgetStartsOnlyAtRollback`, `ExpiryWaitsForActualRollbackAndEveryCleanup_WithoutRetry` and `AllIndependentCleanupRuns_WithOrderedFailuresAndOriginalStack`. Controls cover a noncooperative rollback, timer setup failure, independent captured budgets, no replay, original primary and completion surviving cleanup. Native cancellation feasibility remains W2; public/provider option placement and hosting capture remain W3/W4, without changing this W1 policy.

### F08 — Callback ownership and draining

[TransactionCallbackRunner](../../../../src/DataLinq/Execution/TransactionCallbackRunner.cs) invokes once without occupying the ordinary operation slot. [HelperOwner](../../../../src/DataLinq/Execution/TransactionOperationGate.cs) atomically closes admission and observes active work; it awaits outside the lock and drains each reader once. Any unfinished work prevents commit/result even if it later succeeds. Callback failure precedes unfinished-work and cleanup failures. Confirmed commit is not rewritten by late cancellation.

[Runner tests](../../../../src/DataLinq.Tests.Unit/Core/TransactionCallbackRunnerTests.cs) cover paused commit/cleanup, callback/unfinished-work failures, null tasks, late reader registration, validated pre-cancellation and the distinction between unfinished work and a completed task the caller forgot to await. [Helper lifetime tests](../../../../src/DataLinq.Tests.Unit/Core/TransactionMutationFailureTests.HelperLifetime.cs) cover borrowed completion rejection, escaped async readers between rows or during read/cleanup, and actual pending synchronous readers on LINQ/fluent/raw paths.

`EagerReadFailure_IsObservedBeforeHelperOwnershipHandoff` and `EagerRelationFailure_IsObservedBeforeHelperOwnershipHandoff` in [eager read tests](../../../../src/DataLinq.Tests.Unit/Core/TransactionMutationFailureTests.EagerReads.cs) prove the original failure is captured before the owner transfers. `AsyncCanonical_HelperWaitsForUnfinishedBufferedReadAndRejectsCallbackSuccess` in [canonical loading tests](../../../../src/DataLinq.Tests.Unit/Core/TransactionMutationFailureTests.AsyncCanonicalLoads.cs), the prepared-reader control above, and `AsyncMutation_HelperDrainsUnfinishedMutationAndItsFailureWithoutCommit` in [mutation tests](../../../../src/DataLinq.Tests.Unit/Core/TransactionMutationFailureTests.AsyncMutation.cs) extend that proof to buffered hydration and reserved mutation work. Arbitrary application background work that has not touched the transaction cannot be discovered; later admission is rejected. Public callback overload binding remains W3.
